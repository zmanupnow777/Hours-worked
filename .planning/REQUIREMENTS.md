# REQUIREMENTS

- Support two or more clients with configurable hourly rates.
- Allow one active timer per client and simultaneous timers across different clients.
- Support manual backfill for missed sessions.
- Prevent billed sessions from accidental destructive edits.
- Generate draft invoices for a selected date range.
- Finalize invoices and export PDF + CSV.
- Automate monthly draft creation through a cron-safe endpoint and settings.
